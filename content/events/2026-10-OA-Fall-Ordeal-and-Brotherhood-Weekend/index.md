Brotherhood Inductions

Members who have been Ordeal members for at least 6 months and wish to complete their Brotherhood membership should register as regular attendees and check the box that indicates they wish to complete Brotherhood. The following details the Brotherhood Induction process and you will need to prepare a little in advance.

Have been an Ordeal Member for at least 6 Months
Be currently registered in the Lodge by paying the current year's dues
A fee of $25 for Brotherhood will apply. Please check the box on your registration form. 
Complete the 5 requirements as outlined in the Brotherhood Letter and Study Guide (Document to be sent out before the event, check your emails).
Complete the Brotherhood Hike and Ceremony
