The ideals of Scouting are expressed through the Scout Oath, motto, slogan, and law. These statements and other important information about Scouting are found in the Scouts BSA Handbook.

Troop 924 has developed deep ties with the greater Evanston community. The Troop has partnered with many area non-profit and faith organizations to accomplish projects with a wide array of beneficiaries. Don't be surprised if you see our Scouts removing buckthorn by the canal, cleaning up parking lots, removing litter in forest preserve areas, and serving to honor our veterans. We gladly lend our assistance, and offer service with a smile.
