---
title: Troop Committee
description: Meetings of the adult Troop Committee and its subcommittees.
---

The Troop Committee is the troop's adult board. It sets the budget, coordinates troop activities, and helps the Scoutmaster and the Scouts carry out the program. The full committee meets on the second Monday of each month, 7:30–9:00 PM, and all parents are encouraged to take part.
